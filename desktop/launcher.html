Playing with friends

On the same network, use your local IP. Over the internet, use a public IP or VPN address. Allow Solomon Darker through your firewall and forward the chosen TCP port to the host when needed.

After starting a game, choose Game → Copy multiplayer invite and send it to your friends. Keep the host app open. To switch modes, choose Game → Save and return to launcher.
